Has the AI done what you wanted?

In our experiments, these are a few things the AI never asked about and just assumed:

* which country or countries should the tool work for?
* what should the tool do when an address fails validation?
* should the tool validate private addresses only, or also business addresses? Are PO boxes valid? Are c/o addresses valid?

You can test the tool the AI wrote for you manually. Here are a few starting points:

* Does it recognize your own home address as valid?
* Does it recognize an address with no street name?
* What about an address like "A5, A, 68159 Mannheim, Deutschland"?
